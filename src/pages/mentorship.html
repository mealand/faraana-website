Signature programme

The yearly Vision Board experience.

Every year, Fara’a gathers aspiring leaders and entrepreneurs for a day of reflection, goal-setting and creativity. Participants leave with a physical vision board, a written plan and a community of peers who will hold them accountable.

  • Guided reflection on the past year’s wins and lessons
  • Goal-setting across career, business, faith, health & relationships
  • Hands-on vision-board creation
  • Peer accountability and follow-up mentoring

How mentoring works

Dream. Design. Do. Debrief.

A simple framework that helps young people move from inspiration to measurable progress.

  1. Dream

    Clarify purpose, values and the life you want to build.

  2. Design

    Translate the dream into goals, timelines and a vision board.

  3. Do

    Take small, consistent action with mentoring support.

  4. Debrief

    Review, celebrate wins, learn and reset for the next season.
