Gender-based violence

Standing with survivors, every step of the way.

Domestic abuse thrives in silence, and in remote communities that silence is often deepened by distance, stigma and a lack of services. Fara’a creates safe, confidential spaces where women can speak, be believed and plan a way forward.

  • Confidential counselling for survivors of domestic and sexual abuse
  • Safety planning and protection-focused guidance
  • Referral to medical, legal, shelter and livelihood support
  • Community sensitisation that challenges harmful norms
  • Economic empowerment that reduces dependence on abusers
Need help now? If you are in immediate danger in Nigeria, call the national emergency line 112. For confidential support, reach out through the contact page.

Psychosocial therapy

Healing the person, not just the problem.

As a professionally trained psychologist, Fara’a provides evidence-informed therapy for individuals and groups, especially women who have experienced violence, loss or prolonged stress, and the advocates who carry that weight with them.

One-on-one counselling

A private, judgement-free space to process trauma and rebuild.

Group therapy

Shared healing that turns isolation into solidarity.

Art therapy

Creative expression for what words cannot yet say.

Wellness coaching

Mindset, lifestyle and wellbeing habits for the long run.
