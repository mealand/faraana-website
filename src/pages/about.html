Who she is

From Jaba to Abuja, and everywhere in between.

Fara’a grew up with an early understanding that opportunity is not evenly distributed. Raised with roots in Jaba, Southern Kaduna, and educated at Government Girls’ Secondary School Dutse, she carried that awareness into her studies of law at the University of Jos and criminology & security studies at the National Open University of Nigeria.

Those disciplines (how justice works, why people become vulnerable, and how communities can be kept safe) became the foundation for a career devoted to protection and empowerment. Trained as a psychologist, she pairs that knowledge with professional counselling skills, giving survivors of abuse and people in crisis a safe place to heal.

Alongside her humanitarian and therapeutic work, Fara’a is a natural entrepreneur: a fashion designer and the founder of Farana Empire, a wellness coach, and the innovator behind an agribusiness venture that adds value to Nigerian ginger. She lives what she teaches: that people can build something lasting from whatever they have in their hands.

Speaks English, Hausa & PidginConnects across communities

Career journey

A path built step by deliberate step.

Over 14 years, Fara’a has moved through law, customer experience, enterprise and development practice, collecting the skills that now make her an unusually versatile partner.

  1. 2009 – 2011

    Diploma in Law · University of Jos

    Grounding in legal principles, rights and justice that still shapes her protection and advocacy work.

  2. 2013 – 2015

    Legal Practice Support · Rotimi Ojo & Co. Chambers

    Front-line experience in a law practice: client relations, case administration and confidentiality.

  3. 2015 – 2022

    B.Sc. Criminology & Security Studies · NOUN

    Studied the roots of crime, victimisation and community safety at the National Open University of Nigeria, alongside full-time work.

  4. 2016 – 2017

    Customer Care Representative · Dryclinic Laundry Services

    Built a reputation for building and preserving relationships with customers.

  5. From 2018

    Customer Experience & Operations · Jumia Nigeria

    Supported customers and operations at one of Africa’s largest e-commerce platforms, honing problem-solving, communication and data skills at scale.

  6. Founder

    Farana Empire · Custom Fashion

    Launched a made-to-measure fashion label creating bridal-train, reception, party and girls’ dresses.

  7. Ongoing

    Psychologist & Humanitarian Practitioner

    Counselling, GBV response, community development and women’s economic empowerment in urban and remote rural communities.

  8. Ongoing

    Youth Leader · Young People’s World, ECWA Wuse 2

    Mentoring young adults, organising yearly vision-board sessions and speaking on purpose, leadership and faith.

  9. 2025

    Founder · Nana Ginger Paste

    Developed a naturally preserved, ready-to-use ginger paste to add value to Nigeria’s ginger harvest and create rural jobs.

  10. 2026

    RWVL SafeSpace Intervention & GS-26 Summit

    Took part in group and art therapy for women human rights defenders and in the Policy Innovation Centre’s Gender & Inclusion Summit.
