Agribusiness · Founder

Nana Ginger Paste.

Nigeria is one of the world’s leading producers of ginger, yet most of it leaves the country raw, and families still peel and grind it by hand every day. Fara’a set out to change that.

Nana Ginger Paste is a convenient, ready-to-use ginger paste made with a proprietary, completely natural preservation technique that keeps its aroma, freshness and potency for over a year.

  • Rewards local ginger farmers with steady demand
  • Reduces post-harvest waste through value-addition
  • Creates jobs, with a focus on women and youth
  • Brings an authentic, Afrocentric brand to homes, kitchens and the diaspora
