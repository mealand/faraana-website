Meet Fara’a

A young leader making giant strides, within and beyond her community.

Rooted in Jaba, Southern Kaduna, and based in Abuja, Fara’a Nana Gaji is an independent, career-driven development practitioner whose work moves easily between the boardroom, the counselling room and the village square.

A professionally trained psychologist with a background in law, criminology and security studies, she counsels and protects survivors of domestic abuse, equips rural women to build businesses that last, mentors the next generation of leaders and lends her voice to gender and inclusion policy.

Areas of impact

One mission, six ways of showing up.

Every strand of Fara’a’s work answers the same question: what will help this person, this family, this community stand on their own feet, safely and with dignity?

01

Community Development

Needs assessments, community dialogues and practical programmes for underserved and hard-to-reach communities.

 02

GBV Response & Protection

Confidential counsel, safety planning and protection pathways for women and girls facing domestic abuse and violence.

 03

Psychosocial Therapy

Professional one-on-one counselling, group therapy and art therapy that help people heal and rebuild.

 04

Youth Mentorship

Yearly vision-board programmes and mentoring for aspiring leaders and entrepreneurs, young men and women alike.

 05

Policy & Advocacy

Bringing grassroots evidence into gender, inclusion and protection conversations where decisions are made.

 06

Business Development

An entrepreneur herself, she helps women and youth start, save for and grow sustainable businesses.

The mission

I believe that distance should never decide who gets help. Whether it is a woman finding the courage to leave an abusive home, a mother learning a trade under a village tree, or a young person pinning their dreams to a vision board, every life is worth the journey. My work is to show people the best of what their situation can become, and to walk with them until they get there.

Fara’a Nana Gaji
